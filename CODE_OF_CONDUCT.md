# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

YTlearning is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all community members.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful:** Treat all community members with kindness
- **Be Inclusive:** Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Be Constructive:** Offer helpful feedback without hostility
- **Be Honest:** Share your knowledge authentically

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination
- Hate speech or slurs
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Spam or off-topic content

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation, contact project maintainers with details and relevant links.
