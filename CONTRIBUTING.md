# Contributing to YTlearning

Thank you for your interest in contributing to YTlearning! We welcome feedback, bug reports, and suggestions.

## How to Contribute

### 1. Report a Bug
- Check [existing issues](https://github.com/NexusBT2026/YTlearning/issues) first
- Open a new issue with clear title and description
- Include steps to reproduce and episode reference

### 2. Suggest Improvements
- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/NexusBT2026/YTlearning/discussions) for ideas
- Share what you'd like to see in future series

### 3. Share Your Projects
- Built something with BMAD? Tell us in Discussions!
- Help other learners with their projects

### Community Guidelines
- Be respectful and constructive
- Review the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
