## PRFAQ ANALYSIS

After writing the press release and FAQs, ask yourself:

- Does the press release clearly communicate the value?
- Do the FAQs address the real concerns of your target audience?
- Are there any obvious gaps or missing features mentioned in FAQs?
- Do you feel confident in your answers, or are there doubts?
- Would THIS product solve the problem you described?

**Red Flags to Watch For:**
- ❌ FAQs reveal the problem isn't as big as you thought
- ❌ No good answer to "Why is this better than alternatives?"
- ❌ Target audience is larger than initial brief described
- ❌ You can't explain the value in simple terms
- ❌ FAQs reveal missing features

**Green Flags:**
- ✅ Press release clearly communicates value
- ✅ FAQs are easy to answer
- ✅ Customers would benefit from this
- ✅ You feel confident explaining the project
- ✅ No major feature gaps revealed
