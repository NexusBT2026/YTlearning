## 🎯 Key Concepts

### What is Implementation Readiness?

Implementation Readiness is the final quality gate before starting development. It asks:

- ✅ Is the PRD complete and detailed?
- ✅ Is the Architecture clear?
- ✅ Are all Stories written with acceptance criteria?
- ✅ Is the team aligned on scope?
- ✅ Are there any unresolved risks?
- ✅ Do we have all resources needed?

### The Readiness Checklist

**Product Definition**
- ✅ PRD is complete and signed off
- ✅ All requirements are detailed
- ✅ Constraints and risks are documented

**Technical Design**
- ✅ Architecture is documented
- ✅ Technical decisions are made
- ✅ Scalability is considered

**Story Definition**
- ✅ All stories have clear acceptance criteria
- ✅ Acceptance criteria are specific and measurable
- ✅ Stories are sized appropriately

**Team Alignment**
- ✅ Everyone understands the scope
- ✅ Dependencies are identified
- ✅ Risks are mitigated
